Who we are

Verified leads, sourced by hand

We help agencies, marketers and businesses find their ideal clients through manually researched, verified leads, not scraped, mass-produced lists.

Our work covers Amazon and ecommerce seller leads, B2B prospecting, and specialised niches like solar, investor and homeowner leads, for clients across the USA, Germany, Spain, the UK, France, the Netherlands, Austria, Australia and most other markets worldwide.

Every list goes through manual review before it reaches you, with verified emails to keep bounce rates low. And we always offer a free sample first, so you can check quality and format before committing.
