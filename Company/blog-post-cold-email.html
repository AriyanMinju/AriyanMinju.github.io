Outreach

A simple 3-email sequence that gets replies

By Leads Expert Team

Most cold outreach dies after the first email. Replies usually come from the second or third touch — not the first — so a short sequence beats a single perfect message every time.

Email 1: Specific, not generic

Reference something real about the recipient's business, state what you offer in one line, and end with a low-friction ask like "want a free sample?" instead of a hard sales pitch.

Email 2: Reframe, don't repeat

Sent 3–4 days later, this one restates the offer from a different angle — a stat, a proof point, or a slightly different benefit — rather than just bumping the same email.

Email 3: The graceful exit

About a week after email two, a short "no worries if now isn't the right time" message closes the loop. It often gets replies precisely because it removes pressure.

Keep every email under 100 words. Long emails get skimmed, not read.
