SPF, DKIM & DMARC: why your cold emails bounce

By Leads Expert Team

If your outreach emails aren't landing in inboxes, the problem is often invisible — your domain's email authentication setup, not your message.

SPF (Sender Policy Framework)

Tells receiving servers which mail servers are allowed to send on your domain's behalf. Without it, your emails look easier to spoof — and spam filters notice.

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)

Adds a digital signature to your emails, proving the content wasn't altered in transit and really came from your domain.

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)

Builds on both of the above, telling receiving servers what to do if a message fails SPF or DKIM checks — and gives you visibility into who's sending mail as your domain.

Getting all three set up correctly is one of the highest-leverage, most overlooked fixes for cold email deliverability — often more impactful than rewriting the email itself.
