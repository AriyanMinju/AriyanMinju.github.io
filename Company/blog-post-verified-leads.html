Lead Quality

Why "verified" leads actually convert better

By Leads Expert Team

It's tempting to buy the cheapest, biggest list you can find. More rows feels like more opportunity. In practice, the opposite is usually true: a smaller list of manually verified contacts consistently outperforms a massive scraped one.

Bounce rate kills sender reputation

Every bounced email tells inbox providers your sending domain is unreliable. Enough bounces, and even your good emails start landing in spam — for everyone on your team, not just the campaign that caused it.

Stale data wastes your outreach window

Scraped lists are often months or years out of date. A contact who changed roles or companies isn't just a wasted email — it's a missed opportunity with the wrong message going to the wrong person.

What "manually verified" actually means

For us, it means a person checks the record, confirms the contact still holds that role, and validates the email before it's ever added to a list. It's slower than scraping, but it's the difference between a list that gets replies and one that gets ignored.
