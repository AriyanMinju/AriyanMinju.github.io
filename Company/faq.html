Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our leads, samples, pricing and orders.

Getting started

What kinds of leads do you provide?

Amazon seller and private label leads, ecommerce store owners (Shopify, Etsy, Stripe stores, Otto and Kaufland sellers), B2B decision-makers, real estate, event, investor and venture capital contacts, Women's ERG research, and companies with SPF/DMARC/DKIM or GA4/GTM issues. We also offer research, list building and data services.

Can I get a free sample first?

Yes. Every order starts with a free sample of 10 to 20 leads matched to your criteria, usually delivered within 2 to 3 business days. There's no commitment.

Which countries do you cover?

We work with clients across the USA, UK, Germany, Spain, France, the Netherlands, Austria, Australia and most other markets worldwide. Amazon private label leads are available for the US, UK, Germany, Spain, Australia, France and Mexico.

Can I target a specific city or ZIP code?

Yes. For non-Amazon lead types you can target a city, region or a list of ZIP and postal codes, for example in the US, UK, Australia and Germany.

Data & quality

How do you verify the leads?

A person researches each record, confirms the contact still holds the role, and validates the email before it's added to your list. We never deliver bulk-scraped or auto-generated lists.

What information is included?

The standard layout includes company, website, decision-maker, job title, verified email, phone, country, category and revenue range. You can add LinkedIn URLs, company size, tech stack, social profiles and other custom fields.

What format will I receive?

Your list arrives as a clean spreadsheet: Excel, CSV or Google Sheets, whichever you prefer.

Pricing & orders

How much does it cost?

Amazon seller leads are $280 per 1,000 for the USA and Germany and $320 for the UK. Other lead types start from $180 to $230 per 1,000 depending on the type. The final price depends on your filters, country, data points and volume, and you get a confirmed quote before paying.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, the minimum order is 1,000 leads.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Orders of 5,000+ leads get 15% off, and orders of 10,000+ leads get 20% off.

Can I add personal phone numbers?

Yes, personal phone numbers can be added for $0.10 per lead.

How long does a full order take?

It depends on the volume and how specific your filters are. We confirm a delivery date together with your quote, before you pay.

How do I place an order?

Send your requirements through the request form or contact page. We send a free sample, and once you're happy with the quality we scope and deliver the full order.
