FAQ

Common questions

Can I get a free sample first?

Yes. Every order starts with a free sample of 10 to 20 leads matched to your criteria, usually delivered within 2 to 3 business days. There's no commitment.

How do you verify the leads?

A person researches each record, confirms the contact still holds the role, and validates the email before it's added to your list. We never deliver bulk-scraped or auto-generated lists.

How much does it cost?

Amazon seller leads are $280 per 1,000 for the USA and Germany and $320 for the UK. Other lead types start from $180 to $230 per 1,000 depending on the type. The final price depends on your filters, country, data points and volume, and you get a confirmed quote before paying.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, the minimum order is 1,000 leads.
