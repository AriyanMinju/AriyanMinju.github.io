Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: we only collect what you choose to send through this website, we use it only to answer your request and deliver your order, and we never sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who we are

This website is run by Leads Expert, a lead generation company founded by Kamruzzaman Minju and based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. For any privacy question, email kamruzzamanmju@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact, request and careers forms: your name, email address, company (if given), the lead type you need and anything you write in the message.
  • Chat assistant: the questions you type into the chat window, so the assistant can answer them.
  • Email: anything you send us directly by email.

This website does not use advertising trackers and does not ask for payment details.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and send you a free sample.
  • To prepare a quote and deliver the leads you order.
  • To follow up about your request, unless you tell us not to.

We don't add you to a mailing list or send marketing emails without asking first.

Services that help run this website

  • Web3Forms delivers the website forms to our email inbox.
  • Cloudflare Workers run the chat assistant.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts provides the website's fonts.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We don't share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and order details as long as needed to handle your request and for normal business records, usually no longer than 3 years. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Email kamruzzamanmju@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Security

Forms are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to your information is limited to what's needed to work on your request.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
