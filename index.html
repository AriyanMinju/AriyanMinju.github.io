About

Verified leads, sourced by hand

I help agencies, marketers, and businesses find their ideal clients through manually researched, verified leads — not scraped, mass-produced lists. My work covers Amazon and ecommerce seller leads, B2B prospecting, and specialized niches like solar, investor, and homeowner leads, for clients across the USA, Germany, Spain, UK, France, Netherlands, Austria, Australia, and most other markets worldwide.

Every list I deliver goes through manual review before it reaches you, with verified emails to keep bounce rates low. I offer a free sample before any commitment, so you can check quality and format first.
