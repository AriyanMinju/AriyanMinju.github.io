Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: I only collect what you choose to send me through this website, I use it only to answer your request and deliver your order, and I never sell or rent your details to anyone.

Who I am

This website is run by Kamruzzaman Minju, an independent lead generation specialist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, trading as "Leads Expert". For any privacy question, email kamruzzamanmju@gmail.com.

What information I collect

  • Contact and request forms: your name, email address, the lead type you need and any details you write in the message (for example target country, volume or filters).
  • Order calculator: the options you choose (lead category, country, ZIP codes, quantity). These stay in your browser and are only sent to me if you press a request button and submit the form.
  • Chat assistant: the questions you type into the chat window, so the assistant can answer them.
  • Company Search: the company name or website you search for.
  • AI Lead Planner: the request or business description you type, so the AI can plan your order or suggest your ideal customers. It is not saved unless you send it to me with the request form.
  • Email: anything you send me directly by email.

This website does not use advertising trackers and does not ask for payment details.

How I use it

  • To reply to your message and send you a free sample.
  • To prepare a quote and deliver the leads you order.
  • To follow up about your request, unless you tell me not to.

I do not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing emails without asking first.

Services that help run this website

A few trusted services process data on my behalf, only to make the website work:

  • Web3Forms delivers the contact and request forms to my email inbox.
  • Cloudflare Workers run the chat assistant, the Company Search feature and the AI Lead Planner. The AI Lead Planner uses Cloudflare Workers AI to read your request.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts provides the website's fonts, and Google's favicon service shows company logos in search results.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. I do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Browser storage

The website stores one small setting in your browser for the current visit, so the discount pop-up only shows once. It does not identify you and is cleared when you close the tab.

How long I keep it

I keep messages and order details for as long as needed to handle your request and for normal business records, usually no longer than 3 years. You can ask me to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what information I hold about you,
  • correct it,
  • delete it, or
  • stop contacting you.

Just email kamruzzamanmju@gmail.com and I'll respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Security

Forms are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and I limit access to your information to what is needed to work on your request.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
